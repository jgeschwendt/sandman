Perpetual Shift
Two engines that never stop. The research engine pulls ideas in from the world; the shifts make. The week is the spine: find, restructure, polish, measure, harden, ship, rest. Every decision is an event; revoke one and the machine reconciles. It works the nights; the owner reads the report in the morning and gives the word on what is larger than a shift.
