Cadence — what fires each verb
One lane per trigger. Hooks fire at the session edges, take fires dream, the routines tick on cron, the machine's own sessions call the verbs their prompts name, and the owner calls the rest by hand.
Every arrow is one exec, and no verb polls: each runs, decides, exits. Two stay up — report serve, kept by launchd, and mcp, for as long as its client — and both only answer. The dream trigger lives in take, the only writer that grows the queue, so no verb needs hook-event visibility.
