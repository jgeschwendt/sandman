Flow — a conversation's afterlife
Five stations in the order a conversation passes them, from the ending that archives it to the nightly pass that writes its day — then the three places what it left behind comes back into the next session.
station ①
take
SessionEnd hook · by hand
The transcript moves: a same-volume rename, never a copy; a cross-device destination is an error.
A transcript touched in the last 120 s is refused; --hook implies --force.
The pointer drops, then dream is asked what is undreamed: ≥ 10 spawns a detached dream.
The conversation has left the live, resumable set — the feature, not a side effect.
station ②
archive
bytes at rest
.archive/claude/<yyyy>/<mm>/<dd>/ <HHMMSS>-<relpath>
Plain jsonl, byte-identical: no re-encoding, no compression.
Append-only and never swept: forget destroys before take could see it, so an archived session was wanted.
Un-take is mv back.
station ③
pointer
memories/.recent/<sid>.json
{archived · cwd · ended · title · highlights} — enough to recall by without the transcript.
The short-term surface: recall folds pointers under 72 h into every session start.
Dream stamps dreamed; reflect sweeps those dreamed and ended over 72 h ago. An undreamed pointer never expires.
station ④
dream
3 minds · 2-of-3 · one commit
Oldest ending first, 20 pointers a run; the queue re-read before each one.
sonnet, opus and fable read the same archive in parallel, memory-blind; 300 s each, a failure abstains.
Consensus is integer Jaccard, 2·|∩| ≥ |∪| — no model decides a commit. Under two witnesses, forgotten.
Deduped against the bank, then commit_memory under .commit.lock.
station ⑤
reflect
the 24 h pass · 03:30 UTC
The pending-take drain first: a reclaimed take belongs to the day being written.
The voyage entry for the UTC day that ended — one mind, only when its fingerprint moved.
The pointer sweep; gated bank upkeep, at most 6 ops, never growing a bank.

the next session
sandman recall --hook
Every new session opens on it: one 9,000-character budget over the cwd's bank chain, the pointers, the voyage log tail and the tool index. The same recall answers over MCP, beside remember and banks.
The next session's SessionEnd starts the loop again at ①. forget never enters it: /delete destroys every copy before take could see it.
